# Research backlog — excluded from production

No runtime module imports this directory. The following ideas are proposals,
not calibrated estimates or selectable production models:

1. **Incremental-return estimation:** lagged organic investment and income, with
   acquisition/intangible adjustments and partial pooling. Reject weakly
   identified denominators, unstable coefficients and leakage from holdouts.
2. **Reverse-valuation regions:** combinations of growth, margin and investment
   consistent with price. Preserve multiple solutions; do not turn a surface into
   one preferred growth number.
3. **Cycle/regime scenarios:** joint volume, commodity price, capacity and credit
   dynamics. Require adequate real-cycle evidence and funding constraints.
4. **Robust candidate decisions:** test whether the decision survives supported
   source/parameter uncertainty. Scenario frequency is not probability unless
   out-of-sample calibration supports that interpretation.
5. **Model-disagreement diagnostics:** reconcile DCF, clean-surplus residual income
   and normalized peers before interpreting disagreement. Do not average
   incompatible values or count algebraically equivalent models as independent.
6. **Filing extraction assistance:** source/table citations, exact units and
   accounting tie-outs before an extracted fact enters a model. No inferred
   missing value may be silently treated as reported.

Before promotion, predeclare time/issuer/sector holdouts, baseline methods, stop
conditions, forecast-error and interval-coverage measures, and treatment of failed
or delisted companies. Freeze raw snapshots and configurations. Preserve negative
results. Only later evaluate return predictiveness with realistic costs and an
appropriate benchmark; market-price agreement alone is not validation.
